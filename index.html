What we do

Seven jobs. One team on site.

From the first bucket of soil to the last pour of concrete, VMA Earth Movers handles the groundwork so your build starts on solid footing.

Earth Excavation

Foundations, trenches, pits and basements dug to your depth, width and line.

Concrete Pouring

Footings, slabs, columns and floors poured and levelled to a clean finish.

Centring

Strong shuttering and formwork for slabs, beams and columns, propped to hold the load.

Bricks Supplying

Good quality bricks delivered to your site in loads, ready for the mason.

Land Levelling

Uneven ground graded flat for plots, layouts, roads and construction.

Land Work in JCB

JCB backhoe loader for digging, loading, clearing and all kinds of site work.

Soil Shifting by Lorry

Excavated soil carried away, and filling earth brought in, by lorry loads.
